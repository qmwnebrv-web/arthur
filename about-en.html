About Me

Hey! I'm Arthur Loodus, a 10th grade student who likes to keep life active and interesting. I spend most of my time at the gym or hanging out with friends, but I also enjoy moments when I can just relax and think about my next adventures.

I used to do boxing, which taught me discipline and perseverance – skills I now use daily, whether in training, school, or just getting through life. I also enjoy video games because they provide both fun and challenge.

I consider myself a talkative and social person who always has something to say (sometimes even too much 😄). I value good humor, good friends, and honesty – these are the things that make life worthwhile.

When I'm not at the gym or with friends, I'm probably already thinking about my next goal or just laughing at some silly situation. Life is too short to take too seriously – so I try to find something good in every day.
